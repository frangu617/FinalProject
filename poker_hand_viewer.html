Explore the poker hand dataset

Each row represents five cards and their poker hand category. Labels follow the UCI Poker Hand documentation.

Understand the original dataset codes

S1?S5 mean card 1?5 suit: 1 = Hearts, 2 = Spades, 3 = Diamonds, 4 = Clubs. C1?C5 mean card 1?5 rank: 1 = Ace, 2?10 = numbered cards, 11 = Jack, 12 = Queen, 13 = King.

CLASS means hand category: 0 = High card (UCI: nothing in hand), 1 = One pair, 2 = Two pairs, 3 = Three of a kind, 4 = Straight, 5 = Flush, 6 = Full house, 7 = Four of a kind, 8 = Straight flush, 9 = Royal flush. Royal flush has its own category, separate from straight flush.

Cards stay in their original order. Hover over a value to see its original numeric code. Sorting follows the original codes.
